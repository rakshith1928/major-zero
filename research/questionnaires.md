# Study Questionnaires (T13)

## 1. RedBus familiarity (covariate, before tasks)
1. How often do you book bus tickets online? (never / rarely / monthly / weekly)
2. Have you used RedBus before? (yes / no)
3. How confident are you booking travel online? (1–5)

## 2. SUS — System Usability Scale (after EACH system, 1–5 strongly disagree→agree)
Standard 10-item SUS (Brooke, 1996), scored 0–100 per system.

## 3. Privacy perception (after ZeroBus only, 1–5)
1. I understood what personal details the system stored about me.
2. I felt in control of my stored details (view/delete).
3. I was comfortable letting the system remember another passenger with my permission.
4. Fingerprint login felt more secure than a password.
5. I trust this system with my travel details.

## 4. Debrief (open text)
1. What was the most confusing moment?
2. Did any warning save you from a mistake? Which?
3. One thing to improve?
