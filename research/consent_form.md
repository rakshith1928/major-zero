# Participant Consent Form — ZeroBus User Study

**Project:** AI-Based Smart Bus Ticketing System with Predictive Analytics
(AMC Engineering College, Dept. of CSE-AIML).

**What you will do:** Book bus tickets on two systems (our prototype and the
public RedBus website) following scripted tasks. Your RedBus tasks stop at the
payment screen — you will not pay any money.

**What we record:** Task completion times, number of fields you type, success
or failure per task, your answers to usability and privacy questionnaires, and
your prior familiarity with online bus booking. No passwords, no payment
details, no personal identifiers beyond your name on this form.

**Voluntary:** You may stop at any time, and your data will be deleted on
request. Results are reported only in aggregate (averages, charts); no
individual is identified in the report or paper.
